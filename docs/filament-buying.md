# Filament brands and Amazon listings

Members unlock **Store** with a confirmed donation of at least **$1**, the
smallest amount the donation form accepts. Waiting pledges, unclaimed gifts
and removed gifts do not unlock it. A received gift from any donation bin
counts, including a previous campaign. The printer owner always has access;
their member preview follows the member rule. Library shelf colors remain
available before donating. Store searches and Store filament selections in
uploads, imports and repeat orders enforce the same requirement on the server.

The printer owner manages brands at **Admin → Materials & colors → Manage brands & Amazon links**. Upload an optional logo when adding or editing a brand. PNG, JPEG and WebP uploads under 256 KB are decoded, resized and stored as still WebP images. Known brands have bundled logos from the [Open Filament Database](https://openfilamentdatabase.org/docs); `public/filament-brands/sources.json` records their sources and hashes. Pinball uses the owner's supplied `9346.jpg` artwork, preserved in `public/filament-brands/pinball-source.jpg`. Logo syncs retain this artwork.

To edit an existing brand, tap its current logo to choose a replacement, or tap its name to edit it inline. Name and catalog alias edits save on Enter or when you leave the field; logo changes save when an image is chosen. Escape cancels a name edit. The card shows saving status and validation errors without leaving the page. **Use default logo** restores the bundled logo or initials.

The **Add a listing** form only adds new product/color listings. Existing entries appear in the table below with a color preview, Amazon image, listing price, purchase link and availability controls. **Edit listing** opens a separate edit form below the table. A listing can refer to an existing catalog color or a new product/color entered by the owner. Only enabled brands and colors with exact Amazon product links appear in new orders.

## Smaller packages and the lowest purchase price

Listings can have up to seven alternative packages of the **same brand, product, finish and color**. Use **Other package sizes** to add 500 g half rolls, 250 g quarter rolls, spool refills, 1.75 mm pen refills, or another size. Give each package its exact Amazon URL and a size label. State the whole bundle in the label, such as “250 g × 8 pack (2 kg total)” or “5 m × 20 pen refills”; a multi-color bundle must actually include the selected color. All alternatives use the same Amazon marketplace.

The color picker, spool search and admin table compare each package's full listing price and link to the lowest checked price. A discounted 1 kg roll can still win. Unit prices and calculated prices per roll/kg are never used to choose a package. If some prices cannot be read, the display says **Lowest checked price · some prices unavailable**. If none can be read, the original listing remains the fallback. Shipping, tax and account-specific checkout offers are not available from these page lookups. New requests independently resolve the package on the server and save its URL; existing tickets retain their saved link.

The bundled catalog includes the [SUNLU 250 g eight-color PLA pack](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09TR9FXBL) for its included basic colors and the [SUNLU 500 g white matte PLA refill four-pack](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FC6HQ42G). The quarter-roll pack's selected product page lists black, white, grey, transparent, red, blue/Klein blue, orange/Sunny orange and green. The half-roll listing is a 2 kg pack of four white refills; its identity and size are also recorded by [PriceHistory](https://pricehistory.app/p/sunlu-500g-matte-pla-filament-refill-1-SJPbwESj). These URLs were checked on October 12, 2026. No advertised or historical prices are hardcoded. Pen-refill listings can be saved through the admin form; assorted or random packs are not assigned to exact catalog colors without evidence they contain that shade.

Future palette syncs preserve exact Amazon listings for all published 1.75 mm package sizes, including small rolls and refills, and carry those alternatives across measured/catalog color merging. Discontinued sizes and 2.85 mm filament are excluded. The database migration `20261012140000_filament_package_options` adds package options to existing owner listings with an empty default.

## Images and prices from links

The Store has one rainbow slider to filter the color chart by hue within the current material and finish. It shows nearby shades, including lighter and darker versions and recorded component colors in multicolor filaments. Moving the slider keeps the selected filament unchanged; tap a printed sample to choose it. **All colors** clears the hue filter and restores every shade, including neutral colors.

**Store** shows only filaments with an identified printed swatch, including owner listings linked to an existing swatch. Catalog and custom listings without a swatch are hidden before color deduplication, nearest-color matching and search totals. A swatch whose photo fails to load is hidden from the choices, and store cards do not fall back to product photos. **Library** shows one printed sample per row, using exact product matches when identified and clearly labeled similar color references otherwise. Reference photos retain the sample's own name, maker and material, while the selection retains the saved shelf color. Unknown materials keep their saved preview. Saved request swatches prefer their printed sample and can fall back to the linked Amazon product image. Photos keep their original colors and proportions; no tinting, shared winding texture, simulated finish, or client-side crop is applied. Sample photos use the existing library image route; product photos use the authenticated Amazon image route.

In **Admin → Materials & colors**, the optional **Filament product link** saves an exact Amazon product/color reference for a shelf color; Amazon share links are accepted too. It does not add a spool to the buying catalog. An identified printed sample supplies the Library photo; the saved solid, gradient or Funfetti style still controls the admin preview and future requests.

Glow filaments show a verified **manufacturer glow photo** first, labeled **Glowing**, in the finish picker, color chart, selected preview and saved swatches. The bundled gallery covers all 14 currently enabled, swatched glow colors; `public/filament-glow/sources.json` records each exact sample, manufacturer page, source image and file hashes. Images retain the manufacturer's colors, framing and daylight/glow comparisons. There is no simulated glow or recoloring. If a glow image fails or a newly added filament has no verified glow photo, its usual photo is labeled **Daylight**, with the missing glow photo identified in its description. Glow references are matched by exact sample, maker and material; ordinary shelf colors never borrow a glowing reference because their daylight hex happens to be similar. Glow brightness varies with charging, elapsed time and lighting. `npm run verify:filament-glow` checks coverage, identities, asset hashes and shelf matching.

Paste the exact Amazon product/color listing link. The add form reads the selected product and fills its material, product title, color name and hex when available. Review or adjust the fields, then save; its main image and displayed purchase price appear in the table. No Amazon API account, partner tag or credentials are required. Existing Creators API environment variables are no longer used and can be removed.

Material comes from the product's specifications or title, including common names such as Polylactic Acid → PLA, plus silk/matte and fiber variants. Hex comes from an explicit listing value first, then an unambiguous catalog match for the same Amazon product, brand, material and color. Otherwise a solid color may get an estimate from its product image. The form labels photo estimates so the owner can confirm them; product photography is not a manufacturer color measurement. Clear and multicolor images are not used to invent a single hex. Missing details remain editable, and pulling details does not overwrite fields the owner has already edited or an explicitly selected catalog color.

The page reader checks the selected product's ASIN, including pages whose canonical URL names a parent variant. It uses the main product image and purchase-price markup, with matching Product/Offer structured data as a fallback. It does not run Amazon's scripts. Recommendation prices, crossed-out list prices, unit prices, subscription discounts and marked Prime-only prices are excluded. The displayed price is for the linked listing and its pack size, rather than a calculated price per spool or kilogram.

Successful lookups are cached for 15 minutes; visible pages recheck every five minutes. Up to two product pages are fetched at once. Lookups are restricted to saved listings in the owner's buying catalog, and redirects can only remain on the same Amazon product and marketplace. Images are served through a signed-in route from approved Amazon image hosts. Product pages and images have time and size limits.

Amazon can require a CAPTCHA or block a request, and some pages do not include a readable price. These cases show **Check price on Amazon**; the picker shows **Photo unavailable** if neither sample nor product image can be loaded. The admin table identifies blocked lookups. The app backs off for five minutes instead of trying to bypass the block. An expired cached price is cleared after a failed refresh. Prices can vary by delivery address and change before checkout; the table shows when each price was checked.

Validation: `npm run verify:amazon-products` tests product-page parsing, exact variant identity, currencies, caching, blocked pages, redirect boundaries, response limits, image restrictions, safe logo uploads and bundled asset hashes without network access. `npm run verify:admin-filaments -- --browser` checks actual admin forms, permissions, the listing table, edit controls and responsive layout against a running app.

`npm run verify:filament-packages` checks total purchase costs across half/quarter rolls and pen refills, unreadable prices, currency and marketplace boundaries, exact source variants, source merging and server-selected purchase links. The admin suite also checks saving/reopening packages, rejecting invalid links, disabled-listing price access, authoritative order snapshots and the visible package/price/link agreement.
